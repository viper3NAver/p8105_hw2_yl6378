After cleaning and combining the three Trash Wheel datasets, the final
dataset contains 1,269 observations and 19 variables. The dataset
includes information on dumpster number, month, year, date, trash
weight, volume, counts of different types of waste, homes powered, and
the Trash Wheel source. Rows without dumpster-specific data were
removed, variable names and types were standardized, and a trash_wheel
variable was added to identify whether an observation came from
Mr. Trash Wheel, Professor Trash Wheel, or Gwynnda. For Mr. Trash Wheel,
the number of sports balls was rounded and converted to an integer.
